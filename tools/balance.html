⚖ Balance Simulator

Plays AI-vs-AI duels on the real game rules (loaded from the game itself) for every pair of characters, on both sides of the board. Characters far from 50% overall are flagged. An individual bad matchup is expected; a bad character is not. Results reflect how the AI plays them, not a skilled human.

Tabletop mode runs the counters-and-tiddlywinks rules instead (js/tabletop.js, numbers in tabletop.json, rules in docs/tabletop-rules.md). Tabletop (Beginner) is the same with the Beginner rules: about 12% of flicks pot, you flick 10 minus the spell's cost, and there are no squops. Each mode is balanced on its own.
